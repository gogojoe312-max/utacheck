# v16.40.7 — Compact song toolbar

The v16.40.6 iPhone screenshot still devotes too much height to the status gap
and the two-row header. Combine the title and performance caption into one
picker button, title first, beside the existing direct controls. Apply the same
hierarchy to the overview. Long text wraps, member back remains separate, and
Pro Tools can still insert its control as a direct toolbar child.

Reduce the installed portrait guard from 32px to 20px and header padding from
14px to 8px total. A normal one-line title and caption occupy a 52px toolbar
instead of 78px, yielding 38px more lyric space including the smaller guard.
Long names may increase the toolbar height rather than being clipped.

The 20px guard follows another project's real-device investigation of the same
iOS 27 effect: https://github.com/Yeraze/meshmonitor/pull/5309 . That is supporting
evidence, not a real-device verification of this app. Do not claim the native
blur is gone based on desktop testing.

Validation: existing viewport, keyboard and member navigation checks, plus
published toolbar geometry and picker/overview/settings interactions.
