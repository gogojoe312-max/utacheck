# v16.41.15 — Locate feedback within a song

The member summary previously showed an isolated lyric fragment, making repeated
phrases and short syllables difficult to locate. Each point now includes a small
position label, muted previous/next lyric lines, and the existing highlighted
target. Position and feedback type share a compact header; the repeated
highlight instruction has been removed.

Explicit section names are preserved (for example `2A` becomes `2番・Aメロ`,
`2サビ` becomes `2番・サビ`). `2C` remains `2番・C`; it is not guessed to mean
chorus. Without section metadata, equal lyric lines are labeled by their occurrence
and by lyric-line position. Occurrence counts are never called verse numbers.
Blank rows and section-only headings are excluded from lyric-line counts.
Context surrounds the complete selected range for multi-line notes.

Optional section metadata now survives publication and import, including markers
on blank rows. Deduplication distinguishes songs with different section maps.
Older publications without sections remain readable.
