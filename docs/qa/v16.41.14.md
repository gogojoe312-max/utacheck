# v16.41.14 — Member feedback navigation

Continue the v16.41.13 member-reading work. Selecting a member now retains
feedback addressed to everyone, including legacy records without member IDs,
while excluding another member's personal feedback. Shared notes keep their
audience label in the personal summary.

Summary and comparison links identify the exact feedback record. Opening lyrics
highlights its Unicode character range, or every line of a multi-line note,
instead of only outlining the first row. The range is checked against the song
and performance; invalid old offsets do not highlight unrelated words.
Returning restores the original performance, tab, member and scroll context.
